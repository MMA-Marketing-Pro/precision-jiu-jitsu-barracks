About Precision Jiu-Jitsu.

More than a martial arts academy — a community built on respect, discipline, and a shared passion for growth.

01 — Welcome to our family

A room with real coaches.

At Precision Jiu-Jitsu Spring Mount, we are more than a martial arts academy — we are a community built on respect, discipline, and a shared passion for growth. Whether you're a complete beginner, a seasoned athlete, or a parent looking to enroll your child, you'll find a place here to learn, improve, and thrive.

We offer classes in Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu, Submission Grappling, Strength & Conditioning, MMA Striking, Cardio Kickboxing, Wrestling, and Kids BJJ. Thirty-plus classes a week. Eight instructors. One shared standard — no ego, no intimidation, just mutual respect and a love for the art.

Respect Discipline Growth The Barracks Since 2014
02 — Our Mission

Empower every student who walks in.

Our mission is to empower individuals through the art of Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu. We strive to help every student unlock their full potential — physically, mentally, and emotionally — by providing world-class instruction in a safe, supportive, and inspiring environment.

03 — What Sets Us Apart

Four things you'll feel on day one.

Any gym can hang a sign. What separates an academy is who's teaching, how they teach, and what the room feels like when the warm-up whistle blows. Here's ours.

01 — Expert Instruction

Black belts
with miles on the mat.

Our coaches bring years of experience, competition accolades, and a deep understanding of BJJ fundamentals and advanced techniques. You're learning from people who've put it on the line — not hobbyists rehearsing what they saw online.

02 — Inclusive Environment

Students of every
age and level.

We welcome students of all ages, backgrounds, and fitness levels. No ego, no intimidation — just mutual respect and a love for the art. Kids, women, beginners, competitors — all training under the same roof.

03 — Structured Curriculum

Clear path
from white to black.

We offer clear progression paths for both kids and adults, focusing on skill development, self-defense, and practical application. Every class builds on the last. Every belt means something.

04 — Strong Community

Teammates become
lifelong supporters.

Precision Spring Mount is known for its friendly, family-like atmosphere. Teammates become friends, training partners become lifelong supporters. The mat is where it starts — what builds after class is what keeps people here for years.

04 — Meet the Team

Led by champions. Taught by teachers.

Our head coach Pete Shoemaker is an IBJJF-decorated black belt with a fight-to-win record. Under him: five more black belts, a head wrestling coach, and a pro-MMA Muay Thai specialist. Meet the people who'll be coaching you.
